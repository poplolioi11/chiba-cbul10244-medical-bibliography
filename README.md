# 醫家著述目錄 · Ikachojitsumokuroku

渥見忠篤撰《醫家著述目錄》是《元治増補御書籍目録》的醫家類一冊。全冊錄漢文醫籍的題名、卷冊、著者、鈔刻及版本異同，各類末有數量小計。館目載所屬多冊書目為慶應二年（1866），本冊抄寫年月未載。書目可供追索日本公藏的漢文醫籍及版本流通。這份資料逐幅轉錄千葉大學藏本的 37 幅掃描，以 JSON、TXT 與 TEI XML 保留每幅左右面、書目欄格與定位。全冊 37 幅經兩份獨立的 AI 影像讀稿比對，歧讀回查原圖，待辨處仍有標記。尚待人工全文校對。

Compiled by Atsumi Tadaatsu, the *Ikachojitsumokuroku* is the medical bibliography volume of the expanded *Goshoseki mokuroku*. It records titles, volume and fascicle counts, authors, and edition or manuscript notes for works in literary Chinese. The library links it to an 1866 compilation and leaves this copy’s date unspecified. The dataset covers the 37 source images in JSON, TXT and TEI XML, with a page and column map. Two independent AI image readings were compared and differences checked against the scans. Human proofreading is pending.
